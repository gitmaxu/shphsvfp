# Pesums Browser

**Independent software to end internet surveillance.**

A privacy-first Android browser. Collects nothing. Tracks nothing. Sends nothing.

---

## What Pesums Is

Pesums is built on one rule: everything stays on your device.

Not as a promise. Not as a policy. As a hard technical fact — there is no infrastructure behind Pesums that receives your data. What you browse, search, save, and do is yours alone.

---

## Pesums Shield

The heart of the browser. A full on-device protection system that runs silently in the background on every page, every tab, every session.

- Blocks ads, trackers, and malicious scripts before they ever load
- Stops websites from fingerprinting your device to follow you across the web
- Kills third-party cookies that build profiles on you without consent
- Protects against phishing and malware at the page level
- Works entirely on your device — nothing is sent out for analysis

Shield runs in two modes. **Aggressive** for maximum protection. **Standard** for balanced browsing where some sites need more room. You choose per site, or set a global default and forget it.

## You Control Everything

Pesums puts every setting in your hands. Not buried in menus. Not locked behind toggles you need to hunt for.

- Turn Shield on or off for any individual website without affecting others
- Control cookies globally or per site — block all, allow all, or block trackers only
- Manage JavaScript, pop-ups, and redirects on your own terms
- Full browsing history that lives on your device and nowhere else
- Bookmarks, recent searches, and saved passwords — all local, all encrypted
- Clean tab management with session control

One browser. Total control.

## FAQ

**Why trust Pesums over other privacy browsers?**
Don't take our word for it. The architecture is the guarantee — there is no server built to receive your data. Verify the code when it is open-sourced.

**Does it work without internet?**
History, passwords, bookmarks, and settings all work fully offline.

**Can websites tell I'm using Pesums?**
Basic browser headers are standard. The advanced techniques websites use to fingerprint and track individual users across sessions are blocked.

**Is blocking ads and trackers legal?**
Yes. You have every right to control what runs on your own device.
