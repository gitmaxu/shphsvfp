Pesums collects no personal data. Not your browsing history. Not your searches. Not your passwords. Not your device information. Nothing. This is not a marketing claim it is a technical reality. How Pesums Works Without Your Data

Everything Pesums does, it does locally on your device. Block lists, cookie rules, password storage, browsing history if you choose to keep one, and all settings are stored on your device only in local storage. Pesums does not communicate with Pesums-owned servers to function. There is no round-trip to the cloud. The browser is built offline-first. Your data is yours and goes nowhere.

Technical guarantee: Pesums is architected so that user data never leaves your device. This is not a policy that could be changed later it is how the software is built.

Protection from Online Threats

Pesums actively protects you from a wide range of digital threats. This protection runs entirely on your device no requests are sent to Pesums servers to make blocking decisions.

Pesums Shield

Pesums Shield is our built-in protection layer. All rules are stored locally and enforced locally offline-first, with no cloud dependency.

Ad Blocker

Blocks banner ads, pop-ups, video ads, and native ads using locally stored block lists. This also prevents malvertising malicious ads that can infect your device or steal your data.

Tracker Blocker

Stops tracking scripts from Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, and thousands of other tracking services that follow you across the web. They cannot see you because their requests never complete.

Cookie Protection

Blocks third-party cookies that websites use to track you across different sites. First-party cookies needed for basic site functionality are permitted under your rules. All decisions are made locally.

Anti-Fingerprinting

Prevents websites from building a unique fingerprint of your browser using your fonts, screen resolution, plugins, and other characteristics. Canvas, WebGL, audio, and font fingerprinting are all blocked.

HTTPS Enforcement

Automatically upgrades connections to secure HTTPS wherever possible. This encrypts your traffic between your device and the websites you visit, protecting against eavesdropping and interception. This is enforced locally no Pesums server is involved.

Script Protection

Blocks malicious scripts that websites may attempt to inject into your browser to exploit vulnerabilities or steal data.

Cryptojacking Prevention

Stops websites from secretly using your device's processing power to mine cryptocurrency without your knowledge or consent.

Important: Pesums Shield does not use a live cloud threat database. All block rules are maintained in locally stored lists on your device. Your browsing destinations are never transmitted to any server not even for security purposes.

No Tracking. No Profiling. No Following.

Pesums blocks every known method used to follow you across the web:

  • No cookie tracking: Third-party tracking cookies are completely blocked.
  • No pixel tracking: Invisible tracking pixels are prevented from loading.
  • No browser fingerprinting: Websites cannot create unique identifiers from your browser configuration.
  • No canvas fingerprinting: HTML5 canvas-based tracking is blocked.
  • No WebGL fingerprinting: Graphics card fingerprinting is prevented.
  • No audio fingerprinting: Your audio stack cannot be used to identify you.
  • No font fingerprinting: Your installed fonts remain private.
  • No cross-site tracking: You cannot be followed from one website to another.
  • No behavioral profiling: Your browsing patterns stay entirely on your device.

You Are in Complete Control

Pesums does not collect your data, but you have full control over whatever is stored locally on your device:

  • Clear browsing data anytime: Delete your local history, cache, and cookies whenever you want.
  • Private browsing mode: Browse without saving any local record at all.
  • Auto-delete on close: Set history and cookies to clear automatically when you close the browser.
  • Cookie rules per site: Accept, block, or delete cookies on a site-by-site basis, all enforced locally.

No Third Parties. No Exceptions.

We do not share data with third parties because we have no data to share. No analytics companies. No advertising networks. No data brokers. If a government authority ever presented a legally valid order for user data, we would have nothing to hand over. There is nothing on our side.

Children's Privacy

Open Source & Transparent

Pesums is built on open-source technology. The code is publicly available for inspection. Security researchers, privacy advocates, and developers can verify that Pesums does exactly what it says. No backdoors. No hidden trackers. No secret data collection. You do not have to take our word for it.
