Installing from outside the Play Store

  1. Download the APK above using your phone's browser.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will warn you it's from an unknown source — this is expected for any app installed outside the Play Store.
  3. Tap Settings in that warning, then allow installs from your browser.
  4. Go back and tap Install.

Pesums isn't on the Play Store yet because Google's testing requirements take time to clear. This APK is built from the same source published in our repository.
